# Temporary Frontend Design Review Pages

This folder and route (`/tempPages`) hold alternative frontend design concepts for client review.

## Available Sample Designs

- **Sample Design 1 (`/tempPages/design-1`)**:
  - Inspired by the Mindhouse mental health and mindfulness platform.
  - Implements all 7 reference mockups:
    1. Satin indigo hero with 3 glassmorphic feature cards ("Therapy", "Meditation", "Community").
    2. Interactive "Concerns we address" card slider with illustrated badges.
    3. Deep royal purple transformation section ("Discover your perfect Therapist with Mindhouse") with custom two-silhouette mind-connection visual and 3 key metrics.
    4. "Train your mind" interactive practice widget with tabs (Mindfulness, Breathwork, Sleep Stories, etc.), pastel green benefits banner, and interactive audio player.
    5. Full experience unlock perks list, consultation callback form, and purple wavy "Safe space for sharing" section with anonymous toggle demonstration.
    6. 3-column rounded Testimonials section.
    7. Warm sand/linen split section with asymmetric photo collage and action buttons.
